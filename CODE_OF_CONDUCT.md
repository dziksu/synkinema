# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Explain technical disagreements with
specific evidence and leave room for people with different experience levels.
Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexualized behavior and publishing
someone's private information are not acceptable.

Keep discussions relevant, credit other people's work, and do not post secrets or
private project media. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close hostile
discussions or restrict participation. Repeated or severe misconduct can result in
a ban from project spaces.

For concerns that should not be public, use a private contact channel listed on
the maintainer's GitHub profile. For vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
This policy applies to project issues, pull requests and other official spaces.

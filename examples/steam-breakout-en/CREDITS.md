## Games and official footage

1. **Allumeria** — Infinimelon. Steam Early Access released **28 August 2026**.
   Solo and online co-op; voxel exploration, building, dungeons and boss encounters.
   [Official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3516590/Allumeria/?l=english).
   Source: **Early Access Trailer**, Steam movie **257259073**, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps.
   Only the official HLS video rendition was downloaded; the trailer soundtrack is
   not included in the edit. © Infinimelon.
2. **WheelMates** — Firevolt. Released **1 September 2026**.
   Two-player online/split-screen co-op as tiny RC cars in a professor's house;
   puzzles and traversal depend on cooperation.
   [Official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3905450/WheelMates/?l=english).
   Source: **WheelMates Gameplay Trailer**, Steam movie **257397358**, 1920 × 1080,
   30 fps, video-only HLS rendition. © Firevolt.
3. **Wanderburg** — Randwerk / Sidekick Publishing. Steam Early Access released
   **8 September 2026**. **Single-player** mobile-fortress roguelike, consuming
   villages/fortresses and upgrading modular weapons.
   [Official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3624140/Wanderburg/?l=english).
   Source: **Release Date Announcement Trailer**, Steam movie **257414812**,
   1920 × 1080, 60 fps. Reused the already imported official source; source sound
   is excluded from this composition. © Randwerk / Sidekick Publishing.

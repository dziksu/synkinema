# SpawnBrief — 10-short growth calendar

For new production and revisions, follow [EDITORIAL_RULES.md](EDITORIAL_RULES.md).
The owner's newer situation-led hooks and explicit comment invitations supersede
the older hook/CTA examples below. Do not add an AI-production footer to frames.

Audience: English-speaking players who want a fast, useful reason to care about
new games.  The channel promise remains **quick, no-filler updates on new and
upcoming games**.  Times use Europe/Warsaw (CEST); publish at 18:30 daily for a
consistent first two-week signal.  Recheck every release date and trailer source
72 hours before export because dates can move.

## Channel direction

- Keep the proven 3-game pick format, but alternate it with one-game mechanic
  reveals and deadline/countdown formats so the feed does not look templated.
- The first frame must state the payoff rather than the series name.  Show a
  recognisable game image inside 0.3 seconds; no logo-only opening.
- Cut every 0.8–1.5 seconds.  Put a bass hit on each game reveal, quiet whooshes
  on transitions, and a short riser before a question or release date.
- Use large all-caps phrase captions, not full paragraphs.  Keep the central
  gameplay window bright and reserve the top/bottom for UI copy.
- Use one binary, answerable comment question: a named game, a choice, or a
  prediction.  Do not ask viewers to “like and subscribe”.
- Use only official trailers/gameplay or assets with a documented licence;
  preserve title, store, trailer, music and SFX credits in every Synkinema
  project.  Public availability is not a reuse licence, so each finished draft
  needs rights review before upload.

## Required production checklist for each short

1. Confirm the game, platform, release state and claims against the official
   developer/publisher page and store page on the production day.
2. Use Synkinema MCP to import documented official sources, inspect actual frames,
   generate local narration, add phrase captions, music and licensed/original SFX.
3. Render a preview, inspect hook, title, transitions and CTA at the exact frame
   boundaries; make all visual layers editable.
4. Render final 1080×1920; measure decoded audio, inspect the completed job and
   run speech/caption verification.  Do not schedule a render that fails its
   loudness or visual review.
5. Before upload, write a description with the game facts and source/licence
   attribution.  All hashtags must remain lowercase.

## Initial learning loop

After shorts 1–3 have at least 48 hours of data, compare retention at 1 s / 3 s,
average percentage viewed, swipe-away rate, comments per 1,000 views and the title
card that preceded each result.  Keep the winning hook pattern for shorts 6–10;
do not judge a format by a single hour of views.

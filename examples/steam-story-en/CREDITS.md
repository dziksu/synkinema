returned by MCP `get_steam_games`; source and ownership metadata were preserved.

## Voice

English narration was synthesized locally through MCP using Supertonic 3, voice
M2, speed 1.08 and 12 synthesis steps. The video labels the voice as AI-generated.
Independent `tiny.en` recognition was used for timing checks. Proper names can
produce estimated word alignments; authored captions retain their correct spelling.

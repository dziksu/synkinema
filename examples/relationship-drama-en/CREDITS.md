# Credits and provenance

## Story and audio

**The Girl in His Phone** is an original fictional story generated for this
Synkinema production on 2026-09-12. No Reddit post was copied, and none of the
characters or allegations is presented as a real person or event.

The English narrator is **Supertonic 3, voice F1**, generated locally through
Synkinema. The video visibly discloses its AI voice. The model is distributed
under its [OpenRAIL-M license](https://huggingface.co/supertone-oss-archive/supertonic-3/blob/aafc6e32416a594460b32413efc49d7fe4ce6d46/LICENSE).

The quiet ambient score and notification accents are original additive synthesis
from `compose.py`. No third-party music or sound-effect recordings were sampled.

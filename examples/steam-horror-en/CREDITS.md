## Games and gameplay

1. **the cabin game** — Twisted Corridor👻. Steam **Early Access, 27 August 2026**.
   **1–4 players**, online co-op or solo. Players trapped in a cabin use cards to
   uncover escape clues; card choices change its rooms, events and threats.
   [Official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4406280/the_cabin_game/?l=english).
   Source: **Game Trailer**, Steam movie **257291511**, 1920 × 1080, 24 fps.
   Main final source excerpts: 61–64.8s,79–82.8s,106–109.8s; short additional
   threat shots appear in the hook and closing choices. © Twisted Corridor👻.
2. **Ashes of the Damned: The Forgotten Ward** — Crimson Cloud Games.
   Released **19 August 2026**, **single-player**. An amnesiac protagonist awakens
   in an abandoned psychiatric ward, searches patient records, solves puzzles
   and must flee its entities because there is no combat.
   [Official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3843760/Ashes_of_the_Damned_The_Forgotten_Ward/?l=english).
   Source: **Announcement Trailer**, Steam movie **257391019**, 1920 × 1080,
   30 fps. Main excerpts: 22–26s,34–38s,39–43s. Small brightness correction improves
   visibility without changing the depicted content. The old trailer's “coming
   August / wishlist” ending is excluded. © Crimson Cloud Games.
3. **Halloween: The Game** — IllFonic; IllFonic Publishing / Gun Interactive.
   Released **8 September 2026**. Asymmetric multiplayer: Michael Myers versus
   Haddonfield defenders. Defenders use weapons and household items to fight
   back, alert authorities and rescue families; a separate solo story follows
   Michael Myers. The video does not describe the solo story as co-op.
   [Official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3219630/Halloween_The_Game/?l=english).
   Final source: **Multiplayer Overview Trailer**, Steam movie **257315288**,
   1920 × 1080, 60 fps, downloaded first 100 seconds only. Main excerpts: 38–41.8s,
   74–77.8s,82–85.8s, plus brief hook shots. The final closing choice uses
   source 20.000–21.667s, verified at its start, middle and end to exclude trailer
   title cards. The launch trailer,
   movie 257416913, was inspected and retained as an unused alternate; actual
   gameplay was preferred for this edit. © the respective rights holders.
